## Data Confidence Notes

- **Review counts**: ChatGPT provided Viator/TA numbers. Gemini provided Google numbers. Where the same tour appears in both, counts are additive (different platforms). Some double-counting possible for tours listed on multiple platforms under different names.
- **Ratings**: All sources consistent on ratings (4.8-5.0 for walking tours). Low-rated tours (Hop-On Hop-Off at 3.5) are driving tours, not walking.
- **Addresses**: Verified against known locations where possible. Some stops (e.g., "pizzeria that was mob front") need specific identification.
- **Lat/Lng**: Provided for locations with known exact addresses. TBD entries need geocoding before import.
- **Grok viral analysis**: Based on social media patterns, not quantified metrics. Directionally useful for prioritization, not precise measurement.
- **Tour operator names**: Some generic tours (e.g., "Greenwich Village Walk") may be offered by multiple operators under different brands. ExperienceFirst and Untapped NY appear most frequently as named operators.
