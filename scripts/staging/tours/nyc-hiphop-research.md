## Tour Companies

### Hush Hip Hop Tours (Primary operator)
- Website: https://hushtours.com
- Phone: 212-714-3527
- Established 2000, top-rated on TripAdvisor/Yelp
- Tours led by pioneers and local legends (RayZa, Mike C, Johnny Famous aka Scoob Lover)

**Tour offerings:**
1. **Birthplace of Hip-Hop (Harlem + Bronx bus tour)** -- Covers 1520 Sedgwick, Harlem landmarks
2. **Harlem Hip-Hop Walking Tour** -- Breakdance, beatboxing, street-level history
3. **Where Brooklyn At? (Brooklyn walking tour)** -- Bed-Stuy to Fort Greene, Biggie spots, Jay-Z's Marcy Projects, ODB
4. **Brooklyn Hip-Hop Bus Tour** -- Broader Brooklyn coverage
5. **NYC Hip-Hop Cultural Tour** -- Led by Grandmaster-level guide

## Key Locations NOT Yet in Deep Maps

### Bronx
- **1520 Sedgwick Ave** -- ALREADY COVERED
- **Queensbridge Houses, 10-05 41st Ave, Long Island City** -- Nas grew up in North Houses (18 10th St). Also Mobb Deep, MC Shan, Marley Marl, Roxanne Shante. Coords: ~40.7553, -73.9430

### Brooklyn
- **Marcy Houses, 452 Marcy Ave, Bed-Stuy** -- Jay-Z (Shawn Carter) grew up here. 27 buildings, 1,705 apartments. Coords: 40.6972, -73.9514
- **226 St James Pl, Clinton Hill** -- Biggie. ADDED.
- **Bed-Stuy streets** -- Various Biggie-related spots (corner freestyle locations, music video shoots)

### Staten Island
- **Park Hill Apartments, 160 Park Hill Ave** -- Wu-Tang Clan's "home base." Inspectah Deck, Method Man, Raekwon lived here. "Protect Ya Neck" video filmed outside. Coords: ~40.6235, -74.0780
- **Stapleton Houses** -- Largest SI housing project. Ghostface Killah (Building 218). 693 apartments, 6 buildings. Coords: ~40.6270, -74.0770
- **Targee St & Vanderbilt Ave intersection** -- Officially renamed "Wu-Tang Clan District" in 2019
