### App Status Notes

**Detour (by Bose):** Founded by Groupon co-founder Andrew Mason. Acquired by Bose in April 2018 as part of their "audio AR" initiative. App went free through May 31, 2018, then shut down. Mason pivoted his engineering team to Descript (audio/video editing tool, raised $5M from a16z). Bose abandoned its AR audio platform by summer 2020. No known archive of Detour's tour content exists. The NYC tours (Lower Manhattan, Greenwich Village, Central Park) were considered best-in-class for immersive location-triggered storytelling — a format Deep Maps could learn from.

**Rick Steves Audio Europe:** Confirmed Europe-only. Despite frequent user requests on TripAdvisor/forums for NYC equivalents, Rick Steves has no plans to expand beyond Europe. Gap in the market.

### Key Takeaways

1. **Massive gap in mafia-specific audio/virtual tours.** No platform offers a dedicated NYC mafia self-guided audio tour. Free Tours by Foot has a written self-guided mafia walk but it only covers Little Italy. This is the single biggest content opportunity.

2. **Outer boroughs are a total void.** Zero virtual tour coverage for Brooklyn mafia sites (Gemini Lounge, Galante, DeCicco, Scarpa, DeMeo, Gambino house), Queens sites (Bergin Club, Gotti house, The Hole, Robert's Lounge), or Staten Island sites (Castellano mansion, Cali murder). A Deep Maps "NYC Mafia: Beyond Manhattan" self-guided tour would have zero competition.

3. **Midtown mafia gap.** Even in Manhattan, the Sparks Steak House hit (Castellano, 1985) and the Anastasia barber chair hit (Park Central, 1957) are not on any virtual tour despite being two of the most famous mob hits in history. These are in high-tourist-traffic areas with existing audio tour infrastructure nearby.

4. **Greenwich Village mafia gap.** The Triangle Social Club (Gigante's "Oddfather" base) is walking distance from dozens of Greenwich Village audio tour stops, but no tour mentions it. Every Village tour focuses on literary/bohemian history.

5. **Format precedent is dead.** Detour (the best-in-class immersive audio tour app) is defunct. VoiceMap and Action Tour Guide are the closest active equivalents but neither has mafia content. The immersive, GPS-triggered, narrative-driven format that Detour pioneered is exactly what Deep Maps should build toward.

6. **AR is nascent but coming.** The NYC Revolutionary Trail (launching June 2026) proves AR walking tours are viable and getting investment (Ubisoft partnership). No AR app covers crime/mafia history — another future gap to watch.
