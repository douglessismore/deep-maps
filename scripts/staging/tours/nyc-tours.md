### Tour stops NOT in our database (priority additions)

2. **8 Prince St / Morello HQ** (1 tour) -- MEDIUM. Giuseppe Morello, first boss of what became the Genovese family. Counterfeiting empire HQ ~1900-1910.
3. **9 Prince St / Lupo the Wolf** (1 tour) -- MEDIUM. Ignazio Saietta, Morello's enforcer. Barrel murder cases. Adjacent to Morello HQ — could be one combined moment.
4. **Five Points / 158 Worth St** (1 tour) -- MEDIUM. Pre-Mafia gang era, Dead Rabbits, Whyos. More "dark history" than mafia but foundational.
5. **John's of 12th Street** (1 tour) -- LOW. Restaurant since 1908, atmospheric mob dining spot but no specific major event.
6. **DeRoberti's Pastry** (1 tour) -- LOW. Closed 2014. Atmospheric but no major event.
7. **Elizabeth St social clubs** (1 tour) -- LOW. Generic corridor reference, would need specific club/event to make a moment.
