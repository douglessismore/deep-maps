# Missing Stories Audit — Approach 1: "Stories That Should Exist"

Generated: 2026-03-22
Current inventory: 158 stories, 1,453 moments

## Methodology

Reviewed all 158 existing story names and 23 collections. Identified the 50 most notable missing events/stories based on:
1. Universal recognition — events most educated people worldwide would know
2. Geographic specificity — the WHERE is inseparable from the story
3. "I didn't know that happened HERE" factor
4. Geographic diversity — filling the massive gaps in Asia, Africa, South America, and Oceania
5. Temporal diversity — ancient through modern

## Current Coverage Assessment

**Well-covered regions:** Texas, New Mexico, Mexico, US generally, Holy Land (biblical), Rome, Paris
**Well-covered categories:** US dark history, assassinations, nuclear history, biographies, UFO/paranormal, US true crime
**Major geographic gaps:** East Asia, South Asia, Southeast Asia, sub-Saharan Africa, South America, Oceania, Eastern Europe, Scandinavia, Middle East (non-biblical)
**Major thematic gaps:** Maritime disasters, pandemics/plagues, space exploration (beyond Apollo 13), industrial disasters (non-US), non-Western revolutions, ancient empires (non-Roman), exploration/expeditions

- Many existing biographies (Einstein, Gandhi, Mandela, etc.) touch on events in this list but as biographical moments, not as standalone event stories. The events themselves deserve dedicated story treatment with full moment coverage.

- The Alcatraz story partially exists but could be expanded; same for the Vietnam War if we consider the broader conflict beyond a single incident.
- Collections 6 (genocide sites) and 13 (pandemics) would need sensitive content warnings, consistent with the existing `contentWarning` field pattern used for Tulsa Race Massacre and others.
