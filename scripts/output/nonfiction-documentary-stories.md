## Selection Criteria

Every entry was evaluated on:
1. **Geographic specificity** -- the WHERE is inseparable from the narrative
2. **Moment density** -- the work maps to 3-8 discrete, pin-able events at specific coordinates
3. **Universal recognition** -- how many people would recognize the title
4. **Strotability** -- how many connections each story creates to other stories, people, and places
5. **"I didn't know that happened HERE" factor** -- the location reveal that makes Deep Maps compelling
