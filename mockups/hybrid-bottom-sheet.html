JFK Is Assassinated in Dallas
Kennedy is shot in the presidential motorcade as it passes through Dealey Plaza
1963 Dealey Plaza Dallas, TX
At 12:30 PM on November 22, 1963, three shots are fired from a sixth-floor window of the Texas School Book Depository. President Kennedy is struck in the back and head. Governor John Connally, seated in front, is also wounded. The presidential limousine races to Parkland Memorial Hospital.

Oswald Fires Three Shots from a Sixth-Floor Window
A mail-order Mannlicher-Carcano rifle is found on the sixth floor of the Book Depository
1963 Texas School Book Depository
Lee Harvey Oswald, a 24-year-old former Marine and Soviet defector, fires three shots from a 6.5mm Carcano rifle. Witnesses see a rifle barrel in the sixth-floor window. Within 90 minutes, Oswald is arrested at the Texas Theatre after shooting police officer J.D. Tippit.

Jack Ruby Shoots Oswald on Live Television
Millions of viewers watch as Ruby steps from the crowd in the basement of Dallas Police HQ
1963 Dallas Police HQ
Two days after the assassination, nightclub owner Jack Ruby shoots Lee Harvey Oswald during a prisoner transfer in the basement of Dallas Police headquarters. It is the first murder broadcast live on American television. Oswald dies at Parkland Memorial Hospital — the same hospital where Kennedy was pronounced dead.
